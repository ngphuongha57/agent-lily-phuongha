Why learning sticks · 记忆如何巩固

The video opens with a study of surgical residents and explains that recalling knowledge strengthens memory; sleep may help integrate new learning. 视频以外科住院医师的研究引入，并解释主动回忆能强化记忆；睡眠可能帮助整合新知识。

02:20–03:45

Retrieve and mix · 主动回忆，交错练习

Use flashcards or quizzes instead of relying only on rereading and highlighting. Mix related subjects in one study session to strengthen recall and notice connections. 用闪卡或小测验主动回忆，不要只重读或划重点；在一次学习中交错练习不同主题，帮助巩固记忆并发现联系。

03:46–04:32

Space your reviews · 分散复习

Review across several days rather than cramming. In the example, residents who spread the same amount of practice over four weeks performed better than those who crammed it into one day. 把复习分散到多天，而不是临时突击。视频中的研究显示，把相同练习量分布在四周的住院医师表现更好。

KEY TAKEAWAY · 核心结论
Test yourself, alternate topics, and return to them over time. 用自测检验理解，交错练习不同主题，并隔一段时间再次复习。
